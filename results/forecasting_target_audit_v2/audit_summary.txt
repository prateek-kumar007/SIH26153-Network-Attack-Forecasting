SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The current binary target represents attack presence in a future 30-second window. Because attack episodes can persist across consecutive windows, a persistence baseline can achieve high F1 without demonstrating true pre-attack forecasting ability.

Therefore, high persistence-baseline F1 must not be reported as evidence of successful AI forecasting.

Exact attack onset events should be evaluated separately from general future attack presence. A future attack presence label at +60, +90, +120 or +150 seconds is not automatically an independent attack onset.

Attack-type and attack-family novelty must also be interpreted carefully. The family mapping used by this audit is heuristic and is not an official MITRE taxonomy.
